01 · The proposition

The Mediterranean is a spatial system.

Its most consequential questions do not sit neatly inside administrative boundaries, disciplinary silos, or single datasets.

Land, sea, infrastructure, energy, tourism, environmental pressures, property rights and local communities are increasingly interdependent. Decisions made in one domain alter opportunities and constraints in another.

DELOS is conceived as an independent spatial strategy institute that works at this intersection — bringing advanced geospatial science into the space where institutional choices, investment decisions and public priorities are shaped.

VisionA Mediterranean reference platform for Spatial Intelligence and strategic decision-making.

MissionTranslate advanced geospatial knowledge into evidence, scenarios and actionable strategic frameworks for governments, institutions, markets and society.
02 · From data to decisions

Not another data platform.

The central proposition is to connect spatial evidence with the reasoning required to act on it.

Geospatial dataPlace, time, networks, assets, people, environment
Spatial intelligencePatterns, relationships, semantics, risk and opportunity
Scenarios & evidenceAlternatives made visible, comparable and explainable
Strategic choicesTrade-offs translated into institutional and market language
ImplementationPolicy blueprints, partnerships, pilots and measurable action

This is the role of a boundary organization: not replacing academic research, government or markets, but creating a productive interface between them.

03 · Three domains of action

Where DELOS will work.

PILLAR A

GeoAI & Spatial Decision Support

The challenge

Spatial decisions increasingly involve large, heterogeneous and dynamic datasets — from infrastructure and mobility to climate exposure and urban change.

DELOS approach

Combine GeoAI, spatiotemporal analysis, semantic modelling and visual analytics to turn complex spatial evidence into decision-support environments.

PILLAR B

Blue Economy & Marine Spatial Planning

The challenge

Offshore energy, tourism, conservation, fisheries and coastal communities increasingly compete for the same marine space.

DELOS approach

Support spatially explicit scenarios that make ecological, economic and social trade-offs visible — helping stakeholders move from competing claims toward workable arrangements.

PILLAR C

Land Governance & Property Rights

The challenge

Secure property rights and reliable land information are foundational to investment, planning, infrastructure delivery and institutional trust.

DELOS approach

Build on geospatial governance and cadastral experience to address the spatial foundations of legal certainty, land-use coordination and institutional capacity.

04 · The DELOS difference

A quiet institutional proposition.

DELOS is designed around a simple idea: the value of geospatial intelligence is realized when it changes the quality of a consequential decision.

Action-oriented blueprints

Research is translated into strategic briefs, scenarios, dashboards, maps and implementation frameworks — not left as a technical endpoint.

Translation across worlds

DELOS operates between academia, policy and markets, making assumptions, evidence and constraints legible across institutional cultures.

Spatially explicit incentives

The institute seeks solutions that align economic opportunity, institutional feasibility, environmental resilience and stakeholder acceptance rather than treating them as separate agendas.

DELOS is rooted in a long trajectory at the intersection of geospatial science, institutional governance and applied spatial policy.

Professor Emeritus Marinos Kavouras is a former Dean and Vice-Rector of the National Technical University of Athens and former Vice-President of the Hellenic Cadastre. He has also served in international geospatial research and professional networks, including AGILE.

His work spans Geographic Information Science, cartography, spatiotemporal analysis, ontologies, cybercartography and GeoAI, alongside long-standing involvement in institutional and capacity-building initiatives.

The leadership model is deliberately broader than a conventional academic profile: scientific depth is combined with experience of institutions, policy environments and the practical conditions under which spatial strategies are implemented.

06 · From concept to platform

Building DELOS progressively.

The institute is intended to grow through a combination of focused institutional development, collaborative research and carefully selected strategic engagements.

Rather than fixing a single public funding model, DELOS will use the most appropriate route for each stage and project, while preserving its independence and long-term institutional character.

Institutional development

A focused research and operating core, supported by philanthropic or institutional partners.

Competitive research & innovation funding

European and international programmes for GeoAI, spatial intelligence, climate resilience, marine and land governance.

Strategic partnerships & commissioned work

Selective collaborations with public institutions, research organizations and market actors around clearly defined spatial challenges.

Detailed implementation plans, pilot concepts and project budgets can be developed with prospective partners and funders.
